# S&P 500 MCP Promo Design

## Style Prompt

A bright market-data product promo for S&P 500 MCP. The video should feel like a clean analytics desk crossed with a modern MCP developer console: pale graphite-blue canvas, crisp white data surfaces, warm logo orange, cool cyan/green chart signals, and precise motion that turns scattered market information into one clean AI-accessible data layer.

## Colors

- Background: `#F5F8FC`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Surface soft: `#EDF3F8`
- Surface strong: `#E0E7F0`
- Foreground: `#172033`
- Muted text: `#374151`
- Brand orange: `#9A3412`
- Brand red: `#991B1B`
- Data cyan: `#075985`
- Data green: `#166534`
- Filing amber: `#92400E`
- Risk pink: `#9D174D`

## Typography

- Primary UI and body: `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, system sans-serif.
- Monospace/data: `JetBrains Mono`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`, monospace.
- Headlines use heavy weights and tight tracking; numbers and tool names stay compact and readable.

## Motion

- Market ticks move horizontally with measured momentum.
- Data panels enter with short y/x shifts and scale from their final layout positions.
- Chart paths and bars reveal progressively, then settle.
- Scene transitions use fade, scale, and small directional movement.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make a generic SaaS landing-page video.
- Do not use stock-market imagery that hides the product story.
- Do not rely only on abstract gradients; show tools, MCP Apps, JSON, tickers, charts, and install actions.
- Do not use a dark canvas or dark full-frame background.
- Do not overload the palette with a single blue or purple family.
- Do not animate layout dimensions or use infinite animation loops.
